# Public Goods Funding in Web3: Gitcoin and Rekt News

Public goods are a recurring theme in Web3, yet funding them sustainably remains one of the hardest problems in the ecosystem. This article explores how different projects approach this challenge, focusing on two representative case studies: **Gitcoin** and **Rekt News**.

Rather than proposing a universal model, this research aims to understand where funding comes from, how it is distributed, and what happens after projects receive support.

## Gitcoin: Infrastructure for Funding Public Goods

Gitcoin is one of the most influential public goods funding platforms in the Ethereum ecosystem. It operates at the intersection of open-source development, community governance, and incentive design.

### Core Functions

Gitcoin provides multiple mechanisms to support builders:

- **Grants**: Community-driven donations to open-source projects  
- **Bounties**: Task-based rewards for specific deliverables  
- **Hackathons**: Ecosystem-wide events to encourage experimentation and onboarding  

Among these, **Grants** are the most critical component for long-term public goods funding.

### Quadratic Funding as a Distribution Mechanism

Gitcoin is best known for its use of **Quadratic Funding (QF)**.  
The core idea is simple: the number of contributors matters more than the total amount donated.

This mechanism shifts influence away from large donors and toward broad community support, allowing small contributions to collectively unlock significant matching funds from the treasury.

### Identity and Anti-Sybil Design

To protect funding rounds from manipulation, Gitcoin introduced **Gitcoin Passport**, a decentralized identity system designed to verify that participants are real humans rather than bots or Sybil accounts.

While Passport itself is not a direct revenue source, it plays a critical role in preserving the legitimacy of the funding process.

### Where the Money Comes From

Gitcoin’s funding sources include:

- **DAO Treasury** funded by the GTC governance token  
- **Matching pools** sponsored by ecosystem partners such as foundations and protocols  
- **Platform fees**, including bounty commissions and Grants Stack services  

These sources collectively fund both operations and public goods distribution.

### Sustainability After Funding

A key research question is what happens *after* projects receive Gitcoin funding:

- Do developer communities continue to grow?
- Does protocol usage or TVL increase?
- Can funded projects transition toward independent revenue models?

Gitcoin excels at **initial capital allocation**, but long-term sustainability varies widely across funded projects.

## Rekt News: Public Goods Through Media and Culture

Rekt News represents a very different approach to public goods.

Rather than funding developers directly, it produces **high-quality investigative content** that benefits the entire crypto ecosystem by documenting failures, hacks, and systemic risks.

### What Rekt Produces

Rekt News focuses on:

- Detailed post-mortems of DeFi exploits and security failures  
- Technical storytelling that preserves institutional memory  
- Cultural critique of reckless design and incentives in crypto  

Its articles are widely read by developers, security researchers, and protocol teams.

### Revenue Model: Beyond Donations

Unlike many public goods projects, Rekt does not rely primarily on grants.

Its main revenue sources include:

- **Rekt Drinks**, a consumer product line that generated significant sales volume  
- **Community-backed seed funding**, explicitly rejecting traditional VC investment  
- **Brand-linked crypto assets**, including NFTs and the REKT token  

This model ties public goods production to **brand value and cultural influence**, rather than protocol subsidies.

### Sustainability and Independence

Rekt’s approach allows it to remain editorially independent while maintaining financial sustainability.  
However, it also requires continuous brand relevance and operational execution beyond writing.

## Comparative Observations

Comparing Gitcoin and Rekt reveals two distinct models of public goods sustainability:

- **Gitcoin** focuses on *funding infrastructure* and capital allocation mechanisms  
- **Rekt** focuses on *content, culture, and brand-driven revenue*  

Both serve public interests, but they operate at different layers of the ecosystem.



## Conclusion

Public goods in Web3 do not have a single sustainable model.

Gitcoin demonstrates how incentive design and community coordination can distribute capital at scale.  
Rekt News shows that public goods can also be sustained through market-driven products without relying on perpetual grants.

Understanding these differences is essential for designing future public goods systems that are both impactful and durable.
